Content Lab

Content Lab is a private application that Tom Plumpton runs on his own computer to prepare, publish and review original short videos for his own accounts on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube.

This website provides information about the application and its policies. It does not host the application, and it offers no sign-in. Nobody other than its owner can use Content Lab.

What it does

  • Keeps a local workspace of original videos, captions and titles, and records the owner's review before anything is published.
  • Publishes approved videos to the owner's accounts on a schedule the owner sets.
  • Reads performance figures for those accounts and their posts, so the owner can see what is working.

The accounts are the owner's own: Loop Mechanics on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube, and The Third Door on Instagram.

How it connects to each platform

Instagram

Through the owner's own Meta app, using the Instagram API with Instagram Login. Content Lab publishes Reels to the owner's accounts and reads their posts' insights.

YouTube

A Google sign-in lets Content Lab read the channel's identity and analytics. Videos are published through Zernio, not through that sign-in.

TikTok, and YouTube publishing

Through Zernio, a social media publishing service, where the owner has connected his TikTok and YouTube accounts. Content Lab sends Zernio approved videos to publish.

The records these connections produce stay on the owner's computer. The privacy policy explains what each one accesses, where it is stored and who else receives it, and the data deletion page explains how to remove it.

Google user data

Content Lab uses the YouTube API Services. Content Lab's use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
